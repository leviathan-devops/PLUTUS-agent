# LIQUIDITY SWEEP IDENTIFICATION GUIDE

Liquidity zones (LQZ) are stop-hunt areas where smart money swept resting
orders, then reversed. Three candle types — learn each signature.

## Type 1: LIQUIDITY_SWEEP
- 2 consecutive thick-bodied candles (each body >= 1.5x the recent average body)
  drive into a level.
- A wick pokes >= 2 pips THROUGH the obvious prior low/high.
- Price REVERSES within <= 6 bars.
- The wick extreme is the origin candle — it seeds the week's bias and the
  deepest zone. FIND THE SWEEP FIRST.

## Type 2: LONG_WICK
- Single candle with wick >= 2.5x its body AND wick >= 5 pips.
- Followed by a sharp move AWAY from the wick extreme.
- The wick is the stop raid; the move away is the real intent.

## Type 3: SHORT_WICK_CONSOL
- 3+ consecutive candles whose wick extremes align within <= 2 pips of each
  other (a tight horizontal shelf of wicks/bodies).
- Repeated touches of the same price = a rung of resting liquidity.

## The 2+ Confirmation Rule
ONE pattern type is not enough. A liquidity zone requires 2+ pattern types
aligning on the SAME price line (within ~3 pips), across the lookback.
Example: a sweep wick at 100.945 AND a short-wick consolidation aligned at
100.945 = confirmed LQZ rung. The 3-candle doctrine codifies this: sweep /
long-wick / short-wick-consol, 2+ types on the same line across multiple weeks.

## Where to Hunt
- Just beyond visible swing highs/lows.
- Just beyond round numbers (101.00, 1.1500) — stop magnets.
- Double tops (liquidity above) and double bottoms (liquidity below).
- Trendline breaks — the trap side of the line.

## Mitigation
A swept rung that price has already returned through and closed beyond is
consumed. Fresh, unmitigated rungs are the valid anchors.

## Evidence
Name the types and prices: "liquidity sweep at 100.975 — long lower wick
through 100.945 then sharp reversal up 25 pips in 2 bars; aligned with
short-wick consol at 100.975".
