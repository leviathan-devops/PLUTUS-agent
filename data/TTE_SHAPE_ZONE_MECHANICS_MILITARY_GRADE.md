## THE FUNDAMENTAL TRUTH ABOUT MARKETS

**Markets are algorithmic.** Every candle is produced by institutional algorithms executing mechanical rules. The price action is not random — it follows pre-built algorithmic structure at the macro level.

**TTE shapes are the visible manifestation of these algorithms.** The algorithms create consolidation (SS), then explosive moves (BS/WL), then corrections (R), then liquidity grabs (WL/RWL), then repeat. This is not a pattern — it is the OUTPUT of institutional order flow execution.

**Zones are where the algorithms place their orders.** Supply zones are where institutions sell. Demand zones are where institutions buy. **The shape chain is mechanical and predictable.** SS → BS → R → WL → SS. This is not a probability — it is a SEQUENCE that the algorithms execute. If you can see the SS forming, you KNOW the BS is coming next. If you see the BS hitting a zone, you KNOW the R/WL is coming next.

## THE 6 MACRO SHAPES — COMPLETE MECHANICS

### 1. SS — Swing Scalps (Consolidation)

**What it is:** The "coil" — accumulation phase where price consolidates in a tight horizontal range before a breakout.

**Candle Anatomy:**
- **Count:** 8-30+ candles
- **Body:** TINY (bf 0.10-0.30). Most candles are dojis or spinning tops
- **Total Range:** SMALL. Tight horizontal channel (range ratio to context: 0.2-0.5)
- **Wicks:** MODERATE. Both upper and lower wicks present but not dominant
- **Pattern:** Alternating green/red with overlapping bodies and wicks. "Choppy overlap" — wicks from one candle cover bodies of previous candles. Shows indecision and liquidity accumulation on both sides
- **Direction:** MIXED. Green and red alternate randomly. No sustained direction.
- **Energy:** ACCUMULATING. Price is coiling like a spring, building pressure for release.

**What it LOOKS like:**
- A horizontal rectangle of small, overlapping candles
- Very narrow price range
- Alternating colors with no trend
- Wicks from adjacent candles overlap significantly

**Why it matters:** SS is where the "fuel" builds up. The longer the consolidation, the more powerful the subsequent breakout. A 30-candle SS produces a more violent BS than a 10-candle SS.

**Zone Relationship:**
- SS consolidates BETWEEN a supply zone (above) and a demand zone (below)
- These zones are the anchor points for the SS shape
- The SS will continue until one side BREAKS
- When one side breaks, the BS begins in that direction

**Transition OUT of SS:**
- The SS→BS transition candle is a LARGE MARUBOZU that breaks and closes above/below the SS high/low
- This candle is 3-5x the body size of the SS candles
- It has MINIMAL wicks (near-marubozu)
- It signals the end of consolidation and the start of the breakout

**Direction Determination:**
- If trend is bullish: supply breaks → bullish BS
- If trend is bearish: demand breaks → bearish BS
- If trend is flat: SS continues (no breakout)

### 2. BS — Breakout Surge (Sustained Explosive Move)

**What it is:** The "spear thrust" — a sustained, piercing, directional move that impales through multiple zones with full-bodied momentum.

**Candle Anatomy:**
- **Count:** 4-8+ candles (sometimes up to 10-12 in extended surges)
- **Body:** LARGE on thrust candles (bf 0.65-0.95). Retracement candles have SMALL bodies (bf 0.2-0.4)
- **Total Range:** LARGE on thrust candles (3-5x SS candle range). Retracement candles are SMALL
- **Wicks:** MINIMAL on thrust candles (<0.2 of range). Retracement candles have moderate wicks
- **Pattern:** PROGRESSION from small to large to massive candles. The final candle is the LARGEST (massive marubozu with minimal wicks)
- **Direction:** DOMINANT direction (e.g., all green for bullish, all red for bearish) with occasional small retracements
- **Energy:** PIERCING. Sustained momentum that keeps going and going. Impales through zones with full-bodied candles.

**What it LOOKS like:**
- A sequence of candles that build from small to large to massive
- The thrust candles are full-bodied marubozus with minimal wicks
- Retracement candles are tiny compared to thrust candles
- The final candle is the LARGEST — a massive marubozu that impales through everything
- Sustained momentum — each thrust candle builds on the previous

**Key Features:**
1. **PROGRESSION:** Candles build from small → medium → large → MASSIVE. The final candle is the LARGEST.
2. **Minimal wicks on thrust candles:** The green breakout candles have small wicks (<0.2 of range). The final massive candle has NO upper wick visible.
3. **Retracements are SMALL:** The red retracement candles are tiny (<30% of the thrust candle range). They're just pauses, not reversals.
4. **Direction is clear:** Green candles dominate for bullish BS, red for bearish BS.
5. **Sustained momentum:** Each thrust candle builds on the previous — the final candle is the largest.

**Why it matters:** BS is the main directional move in TTE. It's where the profit comes from. The BS can last an entire session or multiple sessions. It pierces through zones with sustained momentum.

**Zone Relationship:**
- BS + WL moves always move TOWARDS the zone of opposite liquidity
- Bullish BS moves TOWARDS the next supply zone
- Bearish BS moves TOWARDS the next demand zone
- The BS continues until it REACHES that zone
- At the zone: RWL (if unmitigated) or R (if mitigated)

**Zone Type Rules:**
- BS/WL always move to BoM zones, NOT MoM zones
- MoM zones are IGNORED for BS/WL targets — they get pierced and broken
- MoM zones are only anchor points for SS, then they get pierced by BS/WL
- The zone type (BoM vs MoM) determines whether BS/WL will react or pierce

**Transition OUT of BS:**
- BS → R: Exhaustion pin bar (long wick, small body at extreme)
- BS → RWL: Shooting star or bearish engulfing at top of BS
- The transition candle signals that momentum is exhausted

**Energy Mechanics:**
- BS is a "spear thrust" — it pierces through zones with full-bodied candles
- Each candle has a LARGE body and SMALL wicks
- The move is SUSTAINED — each candle builds on the previous
- The final candle is the LARGEST — the explosive impalement

**Direction Determination:**
- If context ends with SS and trend is bullish → predict bullish BS
- If context ends with SS and trend is bearish → predict bearish BS
- If context ends with SS and trend is flat → predict SS (continue consolidating)

### 3. R — Recovery (Correction/Retracement)

**What it is:** The "breathing" — a correction after the BS. A V-shaped retracement where price moves back toward the BS direction but in the opposite direction.

**Candle Anatomy:**
- **Count:** 6-24 candles (often the longest phase)
- **Body:** MEDIUM (bf 0.40-0.60). Moderate body sizes
- **Total Range:** MODERATE (0.8-2.0 of context average)
- **Wicks:** MODERATE. Present but not dominant
- **Pattern:** V-shaped — first half is a trend in the OPPOSITE direction of BS (e.g., if BS was up, R is down). Second half is a recovery back toward the BS direction
- **Direction:** First half: OPPOSITE of BS (correction). Second half: SAME as BS (recovery)
- **Energy:** CORRECTING. Orderly with pullbacks. "Two steps forward, one step back"

**What it LOOKS like:**
- A V-shaped price path
- First half: orderly trend in the opposite direction of BS
- Second half: slower, grinding recovery back toward BS direction
- Mixed colors (green and red alternating with a trend)

**Why it matters:** R is where traders can enter on the retracement for the continuation. It's the "healthy pullback" before the next move.

**Zone Relationship:**
- After BS hits a zone, R is the correction back down
- If the zone was BoM and unmitigated: expect RWL (sharp reversal) instead of R
- If the zone was BoM and mitigated: expect R (soft recovery)
- If the zone was MoM: ignore it, expect BS continuation

**Transition OUT of R:**
- R → WL: Long wick candle at the extreme of the recovery (the liquidity grab)
- R → SS: Return to consolidation if the recovery stalls

**Energy Mechanics:**
- R is the "breathing" after the spear thrust
- First half: orderly correction with moderate candles
- Second half: slower recovery with smaller candles
- "Two steps forward, one step back" — steady with pullbacks

**Direction Determination:**
- If context ends with BS and trend is bullish → predict R (bearish recovery, opposite direction)
- If context ends with BS and trend is bearish → predict R (bullish recovery, opposite direction)
- If context ends with BS and trend is flat → predict R (normal recovery)

### 4. WL — Whiplash (Liquidity Grab)

**What it is:** The "lash from a whip" — an isolated explosive move to grab liquidity at a zone, then immediately reverses.

**Candle Anatomy:**
- **Count:** 3 candles (sometimes 1-2 in the clearest examples)
- **Body:** MODERATE-HIGH on momentum candles (bf 0.5-0.7). MODERATE on grab candles (bf 0.3-0.5)
- **Total Range:** LARGE (2-4x context average). Explosive spike
- **Wicks:** LONG on grab candles (~1x body length). Momentum candles have small wicks
- **Pattern:** Liquidity grab (long wick) → Momentum (large body) → Liquidity grab (long wick). ALL same color
- **Direction:** UNIDIRECTIONAL (all green for bullish WL, all red for bearish WL)
- **Energy:** GRABBING. Whips forward to grab liquidity at zones, with momentum bodies confirming the direction. Dies immediately after.

**What it LOOKS like:**
- 3 candles, ALL same color
- First candle: LONG UPPER WICK (for bullish WL) — the wick is about the same length as the body. This is a liquidity grab to the upside.
- Second candle: LARGE BODY with small wicks — momentum confirming the bullish move
- Third candle: LONG UPPER WICK — another liquidity grab to the upside
- The ENTIRE box is the whiplash shape — not one candle, but a sequence of candles that together form the "lash"

**Key Features:**
1. **ALL candles same color:** Unidirectional. Bullish WL is all green, bearish WL is all red.
2. **Long wicks on grab candles:** The first and third candles have long wicks (~1x body length). These are the liquidity grabs.
3. **Momentum candle in between:** The second candle has a large body with small wicks. This confirms the direction.
4. **Isolated:** Appears, grabs liquidity, dies. No sustained momentum after.
5. **The ENTIRE box is the shape:** Not one candle — a sequence of candles that together form the whip lash.

**Why it matters:** WL grabs liquidity at zones. It's the final explosive move in a direction before pressure is exhausted. Traders use WL to identify where liquidity is being grabbed.

**Zone Relationship:**
- WL + BS moves always move TOWARDS the zone of opposite liquidity
- Bullish WL moves TOWARDS the next supply zone
- Bearish WL moves TOWARDS the next demand zone
- The WL continues until it REACHES that zone
- At the zone: RWL (if unmitigated) or R (if mitigated)

**Zone Type Rules:**
- BS/WL always move to BoM zones, NOT MoM zones
- MoM zones are IGNORED for BS/WL targets — they get pierced and broken
- MoM zones are only anchor points for SS, then they get pierced by BS/WL

**Transition OUT of WL:**
- WL → SS: Momentum is exhausted after the lash. Price consolidates back into SS.
- The WL → SS transition is immediate — the next candles after WL are small consolidation candles.

**Energy Mechanics:**
- WL is a "lash from a whip" — it whips forward to grab liquidity with long wicks
- The wicks grab liquidity at zones (trigger stops)
- The momentum bodies confirm the direction
- Then the energy is SPENT — immediately dies and consolidates

**CRITICAL DISTINCTION FROM BS:**
- BS has NO long wicks on thrust candles — all candles are full-bodied with minimal wicks
- WL has LONG WICKS on grab candles — the liquidity grab candles have long wicks (~1x body)
- BS is SUSTAINED (4-8+ candles building momentum)
- WL is ISOLATED (3 candles: grab → momentum → grab)

**Direction Determination:**
- If context ends with R and trend is bullish → predict WL (bullish liquidity grab)
- If context ends with R and trend is bearish → predict WL (bearish liquidity grab)
- If context ends with R and trend is flat → predict SS (return to consolidation)

### 5. RWL — Reverse Whiplash (Choppy Reversal)

**What it is:** A choppy, violent reversal with liquidity grabs on both sides. The reverse whip lash.

**Candle Anatomy:**
- **Count:** 6+ candles (sometimes up to 14-15 in extended whiplash storms)
- **Body:** MODERATE on all candles (bf 0.3-0.5)
- **Total Range:** MODERATE-LARGE (1.5-3x context average)
- **Wicks:** MODERATE on all candles (~0.3-0.5 of range). Grab candles have longer wicks
- **Pattern:** MIXED colors with the REVERSE direction dominant. Choppy with retracements
- **Direction:** MIXED colors but the REVERSE direction dominates (e.g., if BS was bullish, RWL is predominantly bearish)
- **Energy:** CHOPPY REVERSAL. Violent oscillation with liquidity grabs on both sides. Explosive but with significant retracements.

**What it LOOKS like:**
- 6+ candles, mixed colors (predominantly one direction)
- Choppy price action — explosive but with significant pullbacks
- Some candles have long wicks (liquidity grabs)
- Some candles have moderate bodies (momentum)
- Retracements are MODERATE (30-60% of thrust candle range)

**Key Features:**
1. **MIXED colors:** Not all same color. Predominantly one direction but with retracements.
2. **Choppy:** Explosive but with significant retracements. Not a clean sustained move.
3. **Liquidity grabs on both sides:** Some candles have long wicks (liquidity grabs).
4. **Retracements are MODERATE:** 30-60% of thrust candle range. Bigger than BS retracements.
5. **Shorter and less powerful than BS:** The candles are shorter and less powerful than BS candles.

**Why it matters:** RWL is the reversal after a WL. It's the violent whipsaw where price grabs liquidity on both sides before settling.

**Zone Relationship:**
- RWL happens when BS touches the opposing zone
- If the zone is fully unmitigated: expect RWL (sharp reversal)
- If the zone has been mitigated: expect R (soft recovery)
- RWL happens instantly in the opposite direction when BS touches the opposing zone

**Zone Mitigation Rules:**
- 0-1 mitigations: zone is strong enough to hold
- 2 mitigations: zone is weakening, likely to hold with weak reaction
- 3-4 mitigations: zone is weak and has high likelihood of getting pierced
- Edge case: if zone holds 4+ times and STILL causes strong reactions, it's a fortress zone and will 95% chance flip the direction. This happens <10% of the time.

**Transition OUT of RWL:**
- RWL → SS: After the violent whipsaw, price consolidates back into SS
- RWL → BS: Sometimes the RWL leads to a new breakout surge in the opposite direction

**Energy Mechanics:**
- RWL is a "reverse whip lash" — a violent reversal with liquidity grabs on both sides
- Choppy with significant retracements
- The energy is chaotic, not sustained
- Multiple candles, not just 1-2

**Direction Determination:**
- If context ends with BS and trend is bullish → predict RWL (bearish reversal, opposite direction)
- If context ends with BS and trend is bearish → predict RWL (bullish reversal, opposite direction)
- If context ends with BS and trend is flat → predict R (normal recovery, not RWL)

## THE ZONE-SHAPE RELATIONSHIP — COMPLETE MECHANICS

### Zone Types

**BoM (Beginning of Move):**
- Zone is formed from OB candles at the START of a new move
- Prior move direction is NONE or opposite
- The zone is the FIRST supply/demand in that direction
- BoM zones are the TARGETS for BS/WL moves
- BS/WL will move TOWARDS BoM zones and react to them

**MoM (Middle of Move):**
- Zone is formed from OB candles in the MIDDLE of an existing move
- Prior move direction is the SAME as the zone direction
- The zone is a CONTINUATION of an existing supply/demand
- MoM zones are IGNORED for BS/WL targets — they get pierced and broken
- MoM zones are only anchor points for SS, then they get pierced by BS/WL

**How to Classify:**
- Look at the origin candle for each zone
- If the origin candle is at the START of a new move (prior move is NONE or opposite): BoM
- If the origin candle is in the MIDDLE of an existing move (prior move is SAME direction): MoM
- The origin candle is the OB candle that formed the zone

### Zone Mitigation

**How Mitigation Works:**
- Zones are mapped to the H and L of the origin candle
- If other candles that come AFTER the zone origin candle have their H/L move into this value range, that's mitigation
- In demand zones, the L of other candles mitigates (price dips into the zone)
- In supply zones, the H of other candles mitigates (price spikes into the zone)

**Mitigation Levels:**
- **0 reactions:** UNMITIGATED — zone is strong, never touched
- **1 reaction:** SOFTLY_MITIGATED — zone is still strong, 1 touch
- **2-3 reactions:** MITIGATED — zone is weakening
- **4+ reactions:** HEAVILY_MITIGATED — zone is weak, likely to pierce on next touch

**Mitigation Rules:**
- 0-1 mitigations: zone is strong enough to hold → expect strong reaction (RWL)
- 2 mitigations: zone is weakening, likely to hold with weak reaction → expect soft reaction (R)
- 3-4 mitigations: zone is weak and has high likelihood of getting pierced → expect pierce
- Edge case: if zone holds 4+ times and STILL causes strong reactions, it's a fortress zone and will 95% chance flip the direction. This happens <10% of the time.

**MoM Zone Piercing:**
- If the MoM is fully unmitigated, there will be a MINOR reaction from BS (few small red candles) and then it will pierce through and continue
- This is actually a great entry point for reinforcements — entering on the liquidity zone beneath a MoM because it will cause a minor reaction, sweep LQ, and then continue the BS
- WL will often just pierce through MoM zones without reacting

### The Zone-Shape Chain (Complete Sequence)

**Bullish Example:**

1. **SS Phase:** Price consolidates between supply (above) and demand (below). For bullish trend: supply breaks.

2. **BS Phase:** Bullish BS begins. Continues until it reaches the NEXT 1H supply zone. This must be a **BoM zone**, NOT MoM.

3. **At the Next Supply Zone:**
   - If **fully unmitigated** (never reacted to): expect **RWL** (sharp bearish reversal)
   - If **mitigated** (reacted to previously): expect **R** (soft bearish recovery)

4. **Recovery/RWL Phase:** Price consolidates. Then a **Whiplash (2nd surge attempt)** pushes back to the same zone.

5. **Zone Holds Scenario:** Zone holds → WL energy dissipates → chain resets to SS. Price consolidates between supply and NEW demand (formed at bottom of WL move). Chain repeats.

6. **Zone Breaks Scenario:** Supply gets broken by WL → WL converts to **PBS (Piercing Breakout Surge)** → continues to next supply zone → reaction and consolidation → chain resets.

**Bearish Example:**

1. **SS Phase:** Price consolidates between supply (above) and demand (below). For bearish trend: demand breaks.

2. **BS Phase:** Bearish BS begins. Continues until it reaches the NEXT 1H demand zone. This must be a **BoM zone**, NOT MoM.

3. **At the Next Demand Zone:**
   - If **fully unmitigated** (never reacted to): expect **WL** (bullish whiplash)
   - If **mitigated** (reacted to previously): expect **R** (bullish recovery)

4. **Recovery/WL Phase:** Price consolidates. Then a **Reverse Whiplash (2nd surge attempt)** pushes back to the same zone.

5. **Zone Holds Scenario:** Zone holds → RWL energy dissipates → chain resets to SS. Price consolidates between demand and NEW supply (formed at top of RWL move). Chain repeats.

6. **Zone Breaks Scenario:** Demand gets broken by RWL → RWL converts to **PBS** → continues to next demand zone → reaction and consolidation → chain resets.

## THE SHAPE CHAIN — COMPLETE MECHANICS

### The Master Pattern (Most Frequent)

```
SS (consolidation, 8-30 candles, tiny bodies, tight range)
→ TRANSITION: Large marubozu breakout candle (bf >0.65, minimal wicks, 3-5x SS range)
→ BS (breakout surge, 4-8 candles, large marubozus, minimal wicks, sustained momentum)
→ TRANSITION: Exhaustion pin bar (long wick, small body at extreme)
→ R (recovery, 6-24 candles, medium bodies, V-shaped, orderly pullbacks)
→ TRANSITION: Long wick candle at recovery extreme (liquidity grab)
→ WL (whiplash, 3 candles: grab → momentum → grab, all same color, long wicks)
→ WL → SS: Return to consolidation (momentum exhausted after lash)
→ Back to SS or next BS
```

### The Variations

**Pattern 1: SS → BS → R → WL (Master Pattern)**
- The most common TTE sequence
- SS consolidates, BS breaks out, R corrects, WL grabs liquidity
- Then back to SS for the next cycle

**Pattern 2: SS → BS → RWL → SS**
- BS breaks out, then a violent choppy reversal (RWL)
- Then back to SS
- This is the "failed breakout" pattern — BS surges, RWL reverses violently, SS consolidates

**Pattern 3: BS → R → BS (Double Surge)**
- First BS surges, R corrects, then a SECOND BS surges
- This is the "continuation" pattern — two explosive moves in a row

**Pattern 4: WL → RWL → SS**
- First WL grabs liquidity in one direction
- Then RWL reverses violently in the opposite direction
- Then SS consolidates

**Pattern 5: BS → WLS → SS**
- BS surges, then an extended whiplash storm (WLS)
- Then SS consolidates
- This is the "chaotic" pattern — explosive move followed by extended whipsaw

### The Direction Logic (Corrected)

**SS → BS:**
- SS + trend UP → predict bullish BS
- SS + trend DOWN → predict bearish BS
- SS + trend FLAT → predict SS (continue consolidating)

**BS → R or RWL:**
- BS (bullish) + trend UP → predict R (bearish recovery, opposite direction)
- BS (bullish) + trend DOWN → predict RWL (bearish reverse whiplash, opposite direction)
- BS (bearish) + trend DOWN → predict R (bullish recovery, opposite direction)
- BS (bearish) + trend UP → predict RWL (bullish reverse whiplash, opposite direction)

**WL → SS or RWL:**
- WL (bullish) + trend UP → predict SS (momentum exhausted) or RWL (bearish reversal, opposite direction)
- WL (bearish) + trend DOWN → predict SS or RWL (bullish reversal, opposite direction)

**RWL → SS:**
- RWL + trend ANY → predict SS (momentum exhausted)

**R → WL or SS:**
- R + trend UP → predict WL (bullish liquidity grab) or SS (if trend stalls)
- R + trend DOWN → predict WL (bearish liquidity grab) or SS (if trend stalls)

**WL → SS or BS:**
- WL (bullish) + trend UP → predict SS (momentum exhausted) or BS (if trend continues)
- WL (bearish) + trend DOWN → predict SS or BS (if trend continues)

### The Zone Shape Expectation Logic

**Inside a BoM Supply Zone (unmitigated):**
- expect RWL (bearish reverse whiplash) — sharp reaction to unmitigated supply

**Inside a BoM Supply Zone (mitigated):**
- expect R (bearish recovery) — soft reaction to mitigated supply

**Inside a BoM Demand Zone (unmitigated):**
- expect WL (bullish whiplash) — sharp reaction to unmitigated demand

**Inside a BoM Demand Zone (mitigated):**
- expect R (bullish recovery) — soft reaction to mitigated demand

**Inside a MoM Zone:**
- ignore it, expect BS continuation (pierce through)

**Between Supply and Demand (both nearby):**
- expect SS (consolidation between zones)

**Moving Toward a BoM Zone (dist decreasing):**
- expect BS/WL continuation in that direction

**Far from All Zones (dist > 5):**
- expect trend continuation (BS or R, depends on trend direction)

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **"WL is a small candle"** — WRONG. WL is an explosive move with LARGE total range but the body is moderate relative to the wick. The TOTAL RANGE is large.

2. **"WL has 1-2 candles"** — PARTIALLY TRUE. The clearest WL examples have 3 candles (grab → momentum → grab). Some have 1-2. RWL has 6+.

3. **"Wick is 4-7x body size"** — WRONG. The wick is about 1x body length on grab candles. Not 4-7x.

4. **"bf 0.10-0.25 for WL"** — WRONG. WL momentum candles have bf 0.5-0.7. Grab candles have bf 0.3-0.5.

5. **"BS has all large green candles"** — PARTIALLY TRUE. BS has PROGRESSION from small → large → massive. Retracements are small but present.

6. **"RWL is 1-2 candles"** — WRONG. RWL has 6+ candles. It's a choppy reversal with multiple candles.

7. **"WL is the largest candle in the sequence"** — WRONG. BS candles are usually larger. WL is explosive but BS is usually larger.

8. **"BS retracements are nonexistent"** — WRONG. BS has small retracements (<30% of thrust candle range). They're pauses, not reversals.

9. **"MoM zones are targets for BS/WL"** — WRONG. MoM zones are IGNORED for BS/WL targets. They get pierced and broken. Only BoM zones matter for BS/WL targets.

10. **"Zone mitigation doesn't matter"** — WRONG. Zone mitigation determines reaction strength. Unmitigated zones → RWL (sharp reaction). Mitigated zones → R (soft recovery). After 2 mitigations, zone is weak and likely to pierce on 3rd.

11. **"Zones are passed to candles"** — WRONG. Zones are FIXED POINTS anchored to 1 singular OB candle. They are not passed to other candles.

12. **"Mitigation is the same as touching"** — WRONG. Mitigation means price RETURNS to the zone value range. In demand zones, the L of other candles mitigates (price dips into the zone). In supply zones, the H of other candles mitigates (price spikes into the zone).

**Why this matters:** The model needs to know what shape to expect based on zone context, not just shape chain. For example, if price is inside a BoM supply zone (unmitigated), it should expect RWL (bearish reversal), not just the next shape in the chain.

**The fix:** Add `zone_shape_expectation` as a computed feature that tells the model what shape to expect based on zone type, mitigation, proximity, and trend.

**The fix:** Integrate zone context into the shape chain direction logic. The next shape should be determined by the shape chain + zone context + trend direction.
