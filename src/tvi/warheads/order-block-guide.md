# ORDER BLOCK IDENTIFICATION GUIDE

## What an Order Block IS
The order block is the candle BEFORE a drastic, sharp price move (an
inefficiency). Institutions left unfilled orders in that candle's range; when
price returns, those orders react. The OB zone = the high/low of that candle's
wicks.

## Identification Steps
1. Find a sharp displacement move (large bodies, little overlap, fast travel).
2. Step back ONE candle before the move began — that is the order block.
3. Box the zone from the OB candle's wick high to wick low.
4. Verify the move created a BREAK OF STRUCTURE (higher high or lower low).
   No break = no valid OB.

## Body Criteria (BoM-grade OB)
- Body >= 65% of the candle's total range (body ratio >= 0.65).
- Body-to-wick ratio >= 3.0 (the body dwarfs the wicks).
- A weak-bodied candle before the move is a shelf, not an institutional OB.

## BoM vs MoM
- BoM (Break of Market structure): 2-4 overlapping candle BODIES immediately
  before the displacement that breaks structure. Origin of the move. Strongest.
  4H BoM overrides 1H.
- MoM (Mid-move): a tight consolidation shelf inside an established leg, left
  behind unmitigated. Continuation entry, not origin.

## Unmitigated Priority
- Unmitigated = price has NOT retested the zone since formation. One-time use.
  Strongest possible entry anchor.
- Mitigated = price traded through and closed beyond the zone. Pass-through —
  the orders were consumed. Do NOT enter from a mitigated OB; it is TP
  geometry at best.
- Check: has ANY candle entered the zone since it formed? If not, unmitigated.

## Direction
- Bearish OB (candle before sharp move DOWN) = SUPPLY zone → SHORT entries.
- Bullish OB (candle before sharp move UP) = DEMAND zone → LONG entries.

## Evidence
Name it: "bearish order block at 101.235, body 70% of range, break of
structure below 101.190, unmitigated" — never just "order block".
