the main difference between BS/WL/RWL

BS \= essentially a WL that BREAKS MARKET STRUCTURE. The first surging/piercing impulse that explicitly BREAKS a consolidation. Hence "Breakout Surge" and carries the mose momentum. Very clearly visible  
Often 1-3 BIG FAT dense candles that are very clearly breaking market structure hard and surging the price action towards a new key level. Can also be a set of 6-12 candles that alternate between periods of big fat dense heavy moves and short micro consolidations in between big fat heavy moves. Very distinctly noticeable by big fat heavy candles \+ break of market structure

WL \= a follow up surging impulse that comes AFTER a BS has already happened. Same visual pattern, but often weaker and shorter, on the chart it looks/feels "sudden" or "out of nowhere" \- often happens AFTER a Recovery (R) move that is reversing direction from after the BS move finishes  
(Ex: BS down, R up, WL snaps it back down seeming randomly)  
Often 1-3 big fat vast violent candles, can also something be 4-6 slower candles that look like a SS/R shape but the directional move \+ pressure mechanics behind the candles \+ context to the previous shapes in the chain clearly denote it as a Whiplash shape

RWL \= a whiplash move that instantly immediately reverses the direction from the previous candle violently w/ 0 recovery period. Often this is 1-2 large fat aggressive fast candles, but it can also happen gradually over 4-6 or so smaller candles with the first candle in the RWL shape being the "big aggressive fast reversal" and the rest dissipate more into a SS/R type shape within the RWL. Both are valid
